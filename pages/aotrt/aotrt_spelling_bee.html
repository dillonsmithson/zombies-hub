Skull Hop Step 4

During the fourth step for the Skull Hop Ghost N' Skulls Easter Egg, you'll be presented with a cipher that you'll need to solve to progress. This tool will solve that entire step for you as long as you enter the information in correctly.

To use the tool, simply enter the word from the wall on the Ice Cream Parlor. Then from LEFT TO RIGHT (Left being the symbol closest to Slappy Taffy) Enter each symbol near the swing sets.

Once you've selected your word and symbols, the space below Solution will populate with the solution you can use in game :)
