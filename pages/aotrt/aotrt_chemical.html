Attack Chemical Step Solver

Main Easter Egg

Definitely one of the most complicated zombies easter egg steps, but this tool will make it extremely easy. This tool takes some of the information found from the speedrunning community to make it so that the tool needs a fraction of the information most tools have you enter.

Step 1, you'll enter your M number (found near the pool in the small building) and the Top number of Elvira's TV (the one that says < X). This, in most cases, will calculate your O number just from this, preventing you from having to do the arduous task of finding the O number manually. It will also choose the color you need to view the chem boards in.

Step 2 will have you switch to the color given and look at the Chem board directly outside the TV Station. You'll give the tool the sum of the top and left numbers for Acetaldehyde. In some cases, you'll also need to give the sum of the top and left numbers for Glycerol. From those numbers, it will determine all of your chemical numbers without you having to go and manually collect them.

Step 3 simply has you enter your bomb chemical gathered from listening to the radio. Then it will display the formulas needed to create the chemicals to create your bomb chemical, as well as the numbers you need to punch in to the chemical station!
