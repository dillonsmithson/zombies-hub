Eight Queens Solver

Skull Breaker Step 5

This tool is used during the Skull breaker easter egg in Beast From Beyond. During the step, you'll be presented with a chess board with one queen piece already on the board. The step tasks you with finding 7 pieces around the map, then placing them to solve the famous 8 queens puzzle.

To use the tool, you first need to be viewing the chessboard in game from the correct angle. Standing at the edge of the table, where the chessboard is, and with the chairs surrounding the table on the left and right side of you, view the board from behind the left chair. (Look for the color of the squares, the light color square will be in the left corner) From here, note the starting position of the first queen. Then hit solve and the tool will show you how to place the queens on the board in game.

Note: On mobile, this tool will work some what well. You can use the added buttons above the board to increase/decrease the size of the board. However, the icon for the queen may be a little small. An issue i'm very aware of and will get around to fixing.
